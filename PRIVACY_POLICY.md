## 1. Introduction

Daily Spark ("App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

**Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.** If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

---

## 2. What Information Do We Collect?

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Daily Spark, we collect information you voluntarily provide:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, gender identity, language preference, and authentication credentials (user ID and ID tokens)
- **Quote Preferences:** Your selected content categories, favorite quotes, saved quotes, and collection data
- **Notification Settings:** Your preferences for daily reminders and notification schedules
- **Profile Information:** Display name and user-customized settings (themes, streak tracking)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect certain information about your device and how you use the App:

- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and device settings
- **Usage Data:** Features you access, quotes you interact with (likes, shares, saves), scroll patterns, app session duration
- **Crash Data:** Diagnostic information if the app crashes or encounters errors (via Expo services)
- **Location Information:** None collected by default; notifications are delivered based on your settings, not location

### 2.3 Advertising & Analytics Data

**Google AdMob** collects advertising-related data:
- Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID or IDFA)
- General location (city/country level, not precise)
- App usage patterns for ad targeting and analytics
- Ad impressions and interaction data

**Expo Services** collects:
- App installation and crash diagnostics
- Basic analytics about app usage and stability

## 4. Do We Share Your Information?

### 4.1 Information We Share

We share your information ONLY in these limited cases:

**With Third-Party Service Providers:**
- **Google AdMob** — To display personalized ads. AdMob receives your advertising ID and general usage data. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Expo Services** — For app crash reporting and stability improvements. See [Expo's Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)

**Legal Requirements:**
- If required by law, court order, or governmental request, we may disclose your information to comply with legal obligations

### 4.2 Information We Do NOT Share

We **DO NOT:**
- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your name, email, or account details with advertisers
- Share your saved quotes or personal preferences with any service (they remain private to you)
- Share precise location data
- Share authentication credentials

## 5. How Long Do We Keep Your Information?

- **Account Data:** Retained as long as your account is active. You can request deletion anytime.
- **Saved Quotes:** Retained until you delete them from your collection.
- **Usage Analytics:** Kept for up to 12 months for app improvement purposes.
- **Crash Reports:** Kept for 30 days to diagnose issues.
- **Advertising Data:** Retained per Google AdMob's policies (typically 90 days for older data).

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Access and Control

You have the right to:
- **Access your data:** View your account information, saved quotes, and preferences within the app
- **Update your information:** Modify your name, email, language, content preferences, and notification settings
- **Delete your account:** Request complete deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us
- **Opt out of notifications:** Change notification preferences in Settings at any time

### 6.2 Advertising Choices

You can:
- **Limit ad tracking:** On iOS, use "App Tracking Transparency" settings. On Android, opt out in Google Settings under "Ads"
- **Reset advertising ID:** Reset your device's advertising ID to receive different ads
- **View targeted ads info:** Check your Google Ad Settings to see why you're seeing certain ads

### 6.3 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:
- Know what personal information is collected
- Know whether personal information is sold or disclosed
- Access your personal information
- Request deletion of your personal information
- Opt-out of the selling of personal information (we don't sell data)

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encrypted transmission:** All data between your device and our servers uses HTTPS encryption
- **Secure authentication:** Your password and tokens are securely stored

**However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.** While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.
