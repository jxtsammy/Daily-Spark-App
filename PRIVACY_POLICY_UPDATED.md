We collect information to provide you with the best experience possible:

### Automatically Collected Data
- **IP Address** - For analytics and fraud prevention

- **Timestamps** - When you perform actions in the app

## 6. Data Security

We implement modern security practices to safeguard your data:

- **Encryption in Transit** - All data sent to our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- **Secure Storage** - User authentication tokens stored securely on your device
- **Access Controls** - Only authorized staff can access user data
- **Regular Audits** - Security reviews to identify vulnerabilities
- **No Password Storage** - We use secure authentication tokens, not passwords

**Important:** No digital transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we use industry-standard protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your account access.

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

### For All Users
- **Access** - See what data we collect about you
- **Correct** - Update or fix inaccurate data
- **Delete** - Request deletion of your account and data
- **Opt-out** - Unsubscribe from marketing communications
- **Data Portability** - Receive your data in a portable format

### For EU Users (GDPR)
- **Right to be Forgotten** - Request erasure of all personal data
- **Right to Restrict Processing** - Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Object** - Object to certain processing
- **Right to Lodge Complaint** - File with your data protection authority

### For California Users (CCPA/CPRA)
- **Right to Know** - What personal information is collected
- **Right to Delete** - Request deletion of personal information
- **Right to Opt-Out** - Opt out of "sale" of personal information (we don't sell)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination** - No penalties for exercising rights

### For UK Users (UK GDPR)
- Same rights as EU users apply

### For Virginia Users (VCDPA)
- **Consumer Rights** - Access, correct, delete personal data
- **Opt-Out Rights** - Targeted advertising and sales

**To Exercise Your Rights:**
Contact us at: **support@dailysparkquotes.com**

Include:
- Your name and email
- Which right you want to exercise
- Specific details (e.g., which data)
- We'll respond within 30 days (45 days in EU)

## 8. Children's Privacy

**Daily Spark is not intended for children under 13 years old.**

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA):

- We do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
- If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian before using the app
- If we discover we've collected data from a child under 13, we immediately delete it
- We do NOT serve interest-based advertising to users under 13

**If you believe a child has provided information:**
Contact us immediately at support@dailysparkquotes.com

For users ages 13-17:
- You may need parental consent in some regions
- We limit data collection and ad personalization
- You have full rights to access and delete your data
