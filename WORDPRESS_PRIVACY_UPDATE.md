# Privacy Policy Update Guide

Your WordPress privacy policy needs to be updated to be comprehensive and compliant. Below is the complete, updated privacy policy text to replace your current one.

## How to Update Your WordPress Privacy Policy

1. **Log in to your WordPress dashboard**
   - Go to: dailysparkquotes.com/wp-admin/

2. **Navigate to Privacy Policy page**
   - Click: Pages → Privacy Policy

3. **Delete current content**
   - Select all text (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A)
   - Delete it

4. **Switch to Text Editor** (if using Block Editor)
   - Click the three dots menu → Code editor
   - Or check if there's a "Text" tab

5. **Paste the updated content below**

6. **Click Update** to save

Privacy Policy – Daily Spark

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

Thank you for using Daily Spark — your source of daily motivation and inspiration. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and disclose your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Who We Are

Daily Spark is a mobile application developed by Grete Solutions that provides daily inspirational quotes, motivational content, and personalized experiences to help users stay motivated.

Developer Contact: support@dailysparkquotes.com
Website: https://dailysparkquotes.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide you with the best experience possible:

Personal Information
- Name - When you create an account (optional)
- Email Address - For account recovery and support communications
- Authentication Data - User IDs and session tokens (securely stored)

Usage Data
- App Activity - Features you use, quotes you view, collections you create
- Session Duration - How long you use the app
- Feature Engagement - Which sections you interact with most
- Search Queries - Topics you search for or follow

Device Information
- Device Type - iPhone, Android model
- Operating System - iOS or Android version
- App Version - Which version of Daily Spark you're using
- Unique Device Identifier - For analytics purposes

Advertising Data
- Google Advertising ID - Provided by Google for ad personalization (Android)
- Apple Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) - Provided by Apple for ad personalization (iOS)
- Approximate Location - Country/region based on IP address (for localized ads)
- Ad Interactions - Which ads you view, click, or engage with

Optional User Content
- Feedback - Any comments you voluntarily share about the app
- Reviews - If you leave app store reviews
- Testimonials - If you consent to share your experience

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for:
- Deliver personalized quotes and content
- Send daily reminders and motivational notifications
- Improve app performance and user experience
- Display relevant advertisements
- Create collections and save your favorites
- Communicate with you about new features or support
- Prevent fraud and ensure app security
- Comply with legal obligations
- Analyze trends and improve the app

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

We share limited information only with:

Google AdMob (Google LLC)
- Serves advertisements in the app
- Receives: Advertising ID, approximate location, ad interaction data
- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Expo (Expo Development Company)
- Powers push notifications and app infrastructure
- Receives: User ID, notification preferences, device info
- Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

Vercel
- Hosts our quote database and backend services
- Receives: User ID, authentication tokens, quote data
- Privacy Policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

Analytics Services
- Aggregated, anonymized usage data for improvement
- Cannot identify you individually

Legal Disclosures
We may disclose information if required by law, court orders, or to protect our rights and the rights of users.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary:
- Account information: While account is active
- Usage logs: 90 days
- Crash reports: 30 days
- Advertising data: Per Google/Apple policy
- Backup data: Up to 180 days

You may request deletion of your account and data at any time.

6. Data Security

We implement modern security practices:
- Encryption in Transit - All data uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Secure Storage - Authentication tokens stored securely
- Access Controls - Only authorized staff access user data
- Regular Audits - Security reviews to identify vulnerabilities

No system is 100% secure. Please protect your account access.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:
- Access or correct your data
- Withdraw consent for data processing
- Request deletion of your account and personal data
- Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at: support@dailysparkquotes.com

GDPR Users (EU): You have additional rights including right to be forgotten, restrict processing, and lodge complaints.

CCPA Users (California): You have rights to know, delete, and opt-out of personal information sales (we don't sell).

8. Children's Privacy

Daily Spark is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us immediately at support@dailysparkquotes.com.

We comply with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

9. Permissions We Request

Android:
- POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Send daily reminder notifications
- VIBRATE: Haptic feedback for interactions
- RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Keep reminders working after device restart
- INTERNET: Fetch quotes and serve ads
- READ_PHONE_STATE: Pause ads during calls
- ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Check internet connection

iOS:
- Notifications: Send daily reminders
- Internet: Fetch quotes and serve ads

You can manage permissions in device Settings at any time.

10. Third-Party Services

Google Services
- Google Play Services enables functionality
- Google AdMob serves advertising
- Google Analytics tracks app usage
- Privacy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Apple Services (iOS)

Expo Services

- Analytics and usage tracking
- Privacy: https://expo.dev/privacy

11. Cookies and Tracking

Daily Spark does not use traditional cookies.

We use:
- Mobile Analytics: Understand how you use the app
- Ad Tracking: Through Google AdMob
- Crash Analytics: Fix bugs and improve stability

You can disable ad personalization in your device settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

You will be notified of significant changes through:
- In-app notifications
- Email notification
- Updates to this page

Your continued use of Daily Spark constitutes acceptance of changes.

13. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy?

Email: support@dailysparkquotes.com
Website: https://dailysparkquotes.com
In-App: Settings → Privacy Policy

14. Account Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, contact: support@dailysparkquotes.com
